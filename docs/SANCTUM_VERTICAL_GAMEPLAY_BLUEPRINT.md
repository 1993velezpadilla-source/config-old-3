# SANCTUM OF ASH — Vertical Gameplay Blueprint v1

This blueprint materializes the existing Sanctum Map Bible and Zombies DNA into the current church build. It is an original layout: the reference maps contribute pacing/topology lessons only, never copied room geometry.

## Three-floor combat stack

**Service Undercroft (-1)** is the pressure/power floor. A central boiler/service mass splits movement into two lanes, so the room forms a loop instead of a hallway. One route is available through normal progression; the opposite route becomes a post-power shortcut back into the nave.

**Nave (0)** remains the primary training loop. The current pew/candle pass remains authoritative. New vertical connections sit at opposite sides so the nave does not become a single choke.

**Choir Gallery (+1)** is a U-shaped upper combat route overlooking the nave. It has two independent ways down. It gives sightline advantage but deliberately does not become a safe one-door camping platform.

## Progression

Courtyard / spawn → Nave → Undercroft / Power → powered nave state → Choir Gallery / side progression → tower route.

The upper gallery is optional for raw survival but useful for weapons, a perk, a Mystery Reliquary relocation and quest routing. The undercroft is mandatory because it owns power.

## COD Zombies lessons applied without copying a map

- A readable main loop anchors orientation.
- Side rooms are higher risk than the main arena.
- Vertical spaces use at least two approach/escape paths.
- Power changes navigation as well as audiovisual state.
- Long-term progression physically reconnects earlier spaces.
- Important machines live on natural traversal routes instead of hidden UI menus.
- Major zones expose at least two zombie pressure vectors.
- The player can understand the macro route from landmarks, not arrows.

## Diegetic advertising

Ads are world objects, never interruption UI.

The first three placements are:
- framed wall surface in the nave;
- framed wall surface on the choir gallery;
- spatial radio in the undercroft.

The game never pauses for an ad, never covers the HUD and never requires a click to resume. Radio audio is positional, has a radius/cooldown, and yields to combat/quest dialogue. A provider-policy adapter is required before any third-party network creative is allowed; until then these surfaces support house ads or directly licensed sponsor creatives.

The authoritative machine-readable layout is `docs/sanctum-vertical-layout.v1.json`.
