I made these effects with BandLab. You can recreate your own hits in BandLab like this:

EFFECT SETTINGS:

Graphic EQ 
  
   100Hz = Cut off
   200Hz = Cut off
   400Hz = Unchanged
   800Hz = -5dB (Slightly down)
   1.6kHz & Up = Cut off

BL Driver (Distortion Driver)

   Drive = 0.4
   Tone = 4000Hz
   Volume = Higher or lower depending on your normal sound level
   
4x Univerb

   Atrium, Women's Institute, Dorm Hall, Innovation Hall

Multi Shifter
 
   Octave Down = Full
   Dry = Full
   All other settings off

Attach these effects onto a drum machine, and play different MiDi sounds to get your hits. That was my method - but these effects can also be used on different sounds.

   Also - If you disagree with the pitch sorting of the sounds, feel free to move about the sounds.
   You downloaded the pack - it's yours.
